# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free collaboration space regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and safety risks directly.
- Protect private data and avoid sharing credentials or personal job-search information.
- Accept feedback and focus discussion on the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. After publication, concerns should be reported through the repository's private moderation or security channels. This project does not publish a personal enforcement contact.
